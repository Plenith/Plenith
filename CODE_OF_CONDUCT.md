# Code of Conduct

The Plenith project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version
2.1** as its code of conduct, available at:

> https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

The Covenant defines the pledge, standards, scope, enforcement
responsibilities, and enforcement guidelines that apply to all
project spaces. We follow it as written; this file documents only
the project-specific bits that the Covenant leaves to each project
to fill in.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All repositories under the Plenith project.
- All issue trackers, pull requests, and discussions.
- All real-time communication channels operated under the project
  name (chat rooms, mailing lists, video calls).
- Any in-person or virtual event where someone is officially
  representing the project.

It applies to maintainers, contributors, users opening issues, and
anyone otherwise participating in project spaces.

## How to report a concern

If you experience or witness behavior that violates the Covenant:

1. **Preferred:** email `conduct@plenith.io` with the details.
2. **Alternative:** open a private vulnerability advisory on the
   repository — the same channel used for security reports works for
   conduct reports too. We will route it appropriately.

Please include:

- What happened, with as much specific detail as you can share.
- Where and when (link to the comment / channel / event).
- Whether you'd like to be kept informed of the outcome.
- Any anonymity requirements.

Reports remain confidential within the maintainer team. We will
acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and respond with the
outcome (or a status update) within 14 days.

## Who handles reports

For the 1.x line, the maintainer team is the enforcement body. As
the project grows, we will move to a dedicated conduct committee
with at least one member outside the maintainer team — this is
documented in the governance section of `MISSION.md` and will be
updated here when it happens.

A reporter will never be expected to mediate with the person they
are reporting.

## How decisions are made

We follow the **Enforcement Guidelines** section of the Contributor
Covenant 2.1 (the "Community Impact Guidelines" table) when deciding
on consequences. The four levels described there — Correction,
Warning, Temporary Ban, Permanent Ban — are applied in proportion to
the violation.

We will document the outcome of any non-trivial enforcement action
in a public log (`docs/conduct-log.md` once the first entry exists),
with names redacted where appropriate. Transparency about how this
is being enforced is part of how the policy stays credible.

## Things this Code of Conduct does NOT cover

- **Security vulnerabilities.** Those go through `SECURITY.md`.
- **Technical disagreements.** Strong technical positions, including
  "I think this is the wrong design" or "this approach won't work,"
  are not violations of the Code. *Personal* attacks dressed up as
  technical critique are.
- **Disputes about mission or governance.** The procedure for those
  is in `MISSION.md`.

## Appealing a decision

A person against whom an enforcement action has been taken may
appeal in writing to `conduct@plenith.io`. Appeals are
reviewed by a maintainer who was not involved in the original
decision. The appeal must include a specific reason — new
information, procedural error, or evidence that the consequence is
disproportionate.
